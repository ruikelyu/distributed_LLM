# RQ1 resource footprint: controlled interpretation (v1.0.0)

**Status: approved by the authors for Figure 4 and later coordinated manuscript migration; the manuscript is not edited automatically.**

## Standardized results

- Onsite cooling and humidification water: PF 0.466, LDC aggregate 3.121 million m3/year. The two use different registered WUE scenarios, so the difference is not a causal effect of the architecture itself, and withdrawal and consumption are not distinguished.
- Total facility floor-area demand uses the author-specified central scenario: PF 0.917 million m2; the LDC greenfield project proxy is 1.341 million m2. This comparison represents only the registered central space scenario and is not extrapolated into an unconditional conclusion for other rack densities or site conditions.
- Offsite land conversion: the PF value of 0 is defined solely by the existing-site boundary; the LDC greenfield project proxy is 0.382 million m2. This contrast cannot be interpreted as an empirically observed architecture land effect.

## Field boundaries

- PF new building area remains unquantified; it must not be replaced by 0.
- LDC_AGGREGATE is an equal-service logical capacity portfolio, not one physical data center.
- Floor-area and land intensities are project screening proxies, not national averages or vendor-weighted estimates.
- Cooling scenarios that change PUE are outside this post-processor and require an RQ3 re-solve.
